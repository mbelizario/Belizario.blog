## Blog Test

If you are seeing this, your blog is working correctly.

---

## Purpose of This Post

This post exists to validate multiple aspects of your blog setup. The main goal is to ensure that the Table of Contents (ToC) is working properly, that headings are being detected correctly, and that navigation between sections is smooth.

In addition, this post helps verify visual consistency, spacing, and general readability of long-form content.

---

## Section 1 - Basic Validation

### What is being tested?

We are checking whether the blog:

- Displays headings correctly
- Generates the Table of Contents automatically
- Keeps content structure consistent

### Expected behavior

You should see a Table of Contents with links pointing to each section of this post. Clicking on those links should scroll the page to the corresponding section.

---

## Section 2 - Content Structure

### Organization matters

Even though Hugo uses front matter for ordering, having a clear folder structure helps maintain organization and readability in your project.

### Practical approach

Use a structure that makes sense for you, such as organizing posts by year and month. This does not affect rendering but improves maintainability.

---

## Section 3 - Theme Behavior

### Table of Contents

The Table of Contents is generated based on headings. The deeper the hierarchy, the more detailed the ToC becomes.

### What to observe

- Are all headings listed?
- Is the indentation correct?
- Does navigation feel natural?
